Just mix life-long passion for everything IT, add genuine curiosity and season it with love for facing challenges and finding a way around them.

I'm a Senior IT specialist. Most of my work lives around configuration management and automation: describing systems as code, keeping them reproducible, and taking the repetitive parts off people's hands. Lately that also means putting AI to work where it actually helps and being honest about where it doesn't.

Linux and open source are why I got into all of this in the first place, and I've never really left: I'm a happy GNOME user and a long-time Sailfish OS enthusiast. I created some apps for it — Karuselli, Paperiton, Talteen, Tarkka, Muoto — and I help keep the Italian Sailfish OS community alive over at Jolla Community Italia.
