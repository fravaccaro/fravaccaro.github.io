Perhaps this will be an interesting news for Italian readers only, but the [JollaIt](http://jollait.wordpress.com) website has been totally revamped, featuring a more fresh and clean layout. There you will find breaking news from the Jolla world in Italian, aside from videos and interviews from the web. Alongside a new layout, we are trying to provide a stronger presence throughout the internet, so beside the usual [Twitter](http://twitter.com/JollaIt) account, we have added a brand new [Facebook](http://facebook.com/JollaIt) page. The news are provided by Stefano ([@SteP](http://twitter.com/debexpert)) and me - we are aimed only by the truly enthusiasm for this new Jolla adventure so, even if you are not Italian-speaking, please share us through your favourite social network and - why not? - join us; if you have any question we are glad to answer to you in English as well. Thank you for your support. Be #Unlike.
